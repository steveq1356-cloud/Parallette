About FORM

We build equipment for people who take movement seriously — and refuse to let their environment limit their training.

Where it started

FORM started with a simple frustration: existing parallettes were either cheap and wobbly, or expensive and impossible to travel with. We wanted something that performed at the level serious athletes expect, packed small enough for a carry-on, and looked good enough to leave out in your apartment.

How we build them

Every set is handmade to order. The blocks are formed from a high-performance structural polymer and fitted with a solid poplar hardwood dowel. We don't keep inventory — each pair is made for the person who ordered it.

The philosophy

Calisthenics athletes don't need much — but what they do need has to work perfectly. FORM is built around that idea: fewer compromises, better results. No wobble, no rust, no excuses.

If you have questions or want to talk training, we're easy to reach.
