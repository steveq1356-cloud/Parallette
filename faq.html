Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before ordering.

How strong are they?

Load tested to 400 lb. The blocks are machined from a high-performance structural polymer — the same material used in demanding industrial applications. They will not flex, crack, or deform under normal use.

Which length should I choose?

The Compact (5.5") is ideal for wrist-width push-up and L-sit work. The Extra Long (7.0") gives a wider base for shoulder-width positions and provides more stability during handstand practice. When in doubt, go Compact — it's the most versatile.

Can I travel with these?

Yes, that's exactly what they're designed for. The pair packs flat into a 10"×5"×4" footprint — fits in a gym bag side pocket or carry-on luggage easily.

How do they stay in place?

Each block has four recessed rubber feet that grip any surface — hardwood, tile, gym rubber, concrete. They stay put through L-sits, push-up sets, and handstand holds.

What is the dowel made of?

Solid poplar hardwood, 1⅜" diameter. Poplar is strong, lightweight, and has a natural grain that's comfortable to grip. It develops a nice patina with use.

How long does shipping take?

Production takes 2–3 business days, then standard shipping is 5–7 days ($6). Express is 2–3 days ($14). You'll get a tracking number as soon as it ships.
