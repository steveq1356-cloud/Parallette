Engineered without compromise

Every detail serves a purpose. Nothing is decorative.

Disassembles flat

Slide the dowel free, stack the blocks, and the whole set fits in a laptop sleeve. Goes where you go — gym bag, carry-on, backpack.

Zero-slip base

Recessed rubber feet grip any surface — hardwood, tile, gym rubber, concrete. Stays planted through L-sits, handstands, and push-up variations.

Precision-printed blocks

High-infill 3D-printed structural blocks with a tolerance-fit dowel channel. No wobble, no rattle, no play — just solid contact every time.

Hardwood dowel grip

Solid birch dowel with natural grain texture — warmer and more comfortable than metal, and it only gets better with use as it develops a patina.

Two widths, one purpose

The 5.5" dowel is optimized for wrist-width training. The 7" gives a wider base for shoulder-width exercises and increased stability in static holds.

Five colorways

Choose from Obsidian, Chalk, Crimson, Blush, or Pearl. The block color is yours — the natural birch dowel stays constant across all options.
