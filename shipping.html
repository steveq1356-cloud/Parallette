Shipping info

Every pair is handmade to order. Here's what to expect after you place your order.

Production time

Each set is made after your order is placed. Allow 2–3 business days before your order ships.

Standard shipping

$6 flat rate. Delivered in 5–7 business days via USPS Ground Advantage.

Express shipping

$14 flat rate. Delivered in 2–3 business days via UPS or Priority Mail.

Tracking

You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number as soon as your order is on its way. Check your spam folder if it doesn't show up within 3 business days of ordering.

Domestic only

We currently ship within the United States only. International shipping is something we're working on — check back or get in touch if you're outside the US.

Lost or damaged orders

If your order arrives damaged or goes missing, email us at hello@formparallettes.com with your order number and photos and we'll make it right immediately.
